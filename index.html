INSTITUTIONAL BRIDGE

Not one market. Four connecting mechanisms.

01 Bond Connect

Fixed-income market access connecting international investors and Mainland China through Hong Kong infrastructure.

02 Swap Connect

Cross-border access to onshore CNY interest-rate swaps, linking CFETS, SHCH and OTC Clear.

03 RMB liquidity facilities

HKMA liquidity arrangements supporting intraday and overnight offshore RMB funding conditions.

04 HKD Linked Exchange Rate System

The USD anchor that defines Hong Kong’s monetary boundary while the city intermediates RMB capital flows.

BONDSTATS METHOD

A pressure map, not an FX forecast.

The BondStats Bridge Pressure Index combines HKD peg position, HIBOR, Aggregate Balance, RMB liquidity-facility usage and RMB/HKD momentum. It is a proprietary monitoring composite intended to identify tightening or dislocation across the bridge; it is not an official HKMA, PBOC or HKEX indicator.
